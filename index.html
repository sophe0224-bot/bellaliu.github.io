纪录片拍摄 · 2025年8月Documentary fieldwork · August 2025

可可西里纪录片Kekexili Documentary

环境劳动、地方记忆与人地关系的影像民族志。A visual ethnography of environmental labor, place-based memory, and human-land relations.

在可可西里，我以人类学田野视角观察保护站工作人员的日常巡护、地方知识和环境伦理如何被实践出来。纪录片在这里不只是记录风景，而是一种研究方法：通过影像理解人、制度、动物与高原生态之间持续协商的关系。In Kekexili, I approached documentary filming as anthropological fieldwork, observing how conservation workers practice patrol routines, local knowledge, and environmental ethics. The camera became more than a recording tool: it became a research method for studying the negotiated relationships among people, institutions, animals, and the plateau ecosystem.

研究视角Research Lens 借鉴视觉人类学和生态人类学，关注自然保护如何在日常劳动中被生产出来。Grounded in visual and ecological anthropology, focusing on how conservation is produced through everyday labor.
田野方法Field Method 结合参与式观察、非正式访谈和情境化拍摄，记录巡护现场中的身体经验和地方知识。Combined participant observation, informal interviews, and situated filming to capture embodied experience and local knowledge.
核心问题Core Question 保护者如何把抽象的生态责任转化为可执行、可传承、可被公众理解的社会实践？How do conservation workers turn abstract ecological responsibility into social practice that can be acted on, remembered, and shared?
思想收获Conceptual Takeaway 环境保护不是单纯的自然管理，而是人与非人生命、制度和记忆共同构成的文化实践。Environmental protection is not only nature management; it is a cultural practice shaped by humans, nonhuman life, institutions, and memory.

支教服务 · 2025年6月Teaching service · June 2025

斯里兰卡融合教育服务Sri Lanka Inclusive Education Service

沟通、无障碍和以人为中心的学习。Communication, accessibility, and human-centered learning.

和斯里兰卡残障儿童一起工作让我明白，沟通并不只靠语言。艺术、动作、手势和游戏都可以成为跨越语言、能力和文化的桥梁，也让我开始关注 AI 如何支持包容教育：帮助表达，但不替代人的关怀。Working with children with disabilities in Sri Lanka taught me that communication is not only verbal. Art, movement, gesture, and play can become bridges across language, ability, and culture. This experience shaped my interest in AI for inclusive education: tools that support expression without replacing human care.
